About FarmCult

FarmCult is a smart agricultural guidance platform designed to help farmers choose the right crops based on soil type, climate, rainfall, and environmental conditions.

We combine traditional farming knowledge with modern technology to support sustainable agriculture and maximize crop yield.

🌱 Our Mission

To empower farmers with data-driven crop recommendations, reduce farming risks, improve productivity, and promote eco-friendly agricultural practices for a better future.
